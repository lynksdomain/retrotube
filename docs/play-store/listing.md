## Full description (max 4000 characters)

RetroTube plays your own video library through real, RetroArch-style CRT shaders — scanlines, phosphor mask, chroma bleed, geometry warp — without configuring RetroArch or an emulator core just to watch a video that looks right.

Point it at a folder (or a network share) and every video in it plays back through the shader pipeline. Pick a look once as your default, or override it per file. Built for the retro handheld crowd, but it's just a video player underneath — anything with a folder of video files works.

REAL CRT SHADING
• 7 shader presets across 3 device-cost tiers, each modeled on a real RetroArch preset family — zfast-crt, phosphor-mono, deconverge, crt-easymode, vhs, crt-guest-advanced, ntsc
• Independent curvature toggle — combine screen warp with any preset, or none
• Resolution downscale pre-pass with integer-scale snapping, so scanlines read as chunky and authentic instead of a faint HD filter
• Fit / stretch / crop aspect modes
• Global default + per-file override, plus an instant raw/shaded A-B compare

A REAL FOLDER-TREE LIBRARY
• Browse your own folder structure — not a flattened dump of every file
• Connect an SMB share and browse a NAS or another computer the same way
• Custom titles, tags, and poster art per video
• Collections — hand-curated shelves pulling from anywhere in your library
• Continue Watching, automatically, with resume-from-position everywhere

TV MODE
Flip through channels like a real TV, built out of your own library:
• You program the channels — auto-generate a starting lineup or build it from scratch, from whole folders down to individual nested files
• No transport controls, just channel up/down and power — like a real remote
• A beat of real tuner static between channels, with a CRT-style channel readout drawn through the static
• Resume-aware, without touching your normal Continue Watching

BUILT FOR HANDHELDS
• True fullscreen playback
• Gamepad shoulder-button navigation (channel up/down in TV Mode, previous/next video otherwise)

RetroTube is free, open source (GPL-3.0), and has no ads, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything it does happens on your device.
